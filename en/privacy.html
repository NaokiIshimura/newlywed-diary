Privacy policy

This policy explains how Newlywed Diary (https://newlywed-diary.nacn.app/, "this game") handles information about its users.

Information stored in your browser

This game stores the following information, which it needs in order to be played, only in your browser (localStorage). It is never sent to our servers.

  • Your save in progress: your starting settings (gender, age, savings, income and work style) and your game progress (the choices you made, the diary and your household finances)
  • Your endings and achievements record: the endings, titles and achievements you've seen, how many times you've played, and your best net worth
  • Settings: whether to show year-end finances, and the default speed
  • Your chosen language (lang) and whether you closed the language suggestion (lang-suggest-closed). These are shared across all nacn.app sites

You can erase this information with "Delete data" in Settings, or by clearing your browser data.

Sharing your results

The image for sharing your results is created inside your browser. When you use "Share image" or "Post to X (Twitter)", the image and text are sent only to the service you choose to share to.

Analytics

This game uses Google Analytics, an analytics tool by Google, to understand how the game is used and to improve it. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect traffic data. This data is collected anonymously and does not identify you personally. The choices you make in the game and your results are never sent to Google Analytics.

You can refuse this collection by disabling cookies in your browser settings. You can also use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. For how this data is handled, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

This game does not show ads.

Cookie consent

For visitors from countries where prior consent is required, such as Indonesia, Thailand, and Vietnam, we show a cookie consent notice at the bottom of the screen and do not use Google Analytics cookies until you agree. Until then, basic measurements that do not use cookies (such as the fact that a page was opened) are still sent to Google. For visitors from the EU, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the game is shown with cookies turned off.

Your choice is saved in a cookie shared by all nacn.app sites (nacn_consent, kept for about 13 months). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the title screen.

Information you send through the contact form

Information you send through the contact form (Google Forms), such as your message and email address, is used only to reply to you and is not used for any other purpose.

Notice for U.S. residents

We do not sell or share your personal information. This game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Disclaimer

The incomes, living costs, education costs, housing costs and other amounts in this game are simplified estimates for gameplay, based on public statistics and typical market prices. They don't include pensions, inflation or detailed tax calculations, and they do not represent actual household finances or investment results. For household budgets, mortgages or education savings plans, please consult a professional such as a financial planner. We are not responsible for any damages arising from the use of this game.

Operator and contact

This game is operated by nacn. For the operator and how to contact us, see About & Contact on nacn.app. Please also see the Privacy Policy shared by nacn.app and the games published on its subdomains.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be changed without notice as needed. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.
